ICC rules built into this scorer

Runs and extras

  • Wide: 1 penalty run plus any runs run; not a legal ball, not faced by the batter, charged to the bowler. A wide that reaches the boundary scores 5.
  • No-ball: 1 penalty run plus runs off the bat (to the batter) or byes / leg-byes (to extras); not a legal ball; charged to the bowler.
  • Byes and leg-byes: extras only. Not charged to the bowler, not credited to the batter.
  • Penalty runs: 5 runs added as penalty extras.
  • Batters change ends on odd runs (not on boundaries) and at the end of every over.

Free hit (T20, ODI, custom overs)

  • The ball after a no-ball is a free hit. Wides do not use it up; another no-ball gives another free hit.
  • On a free hit the batter can only be out run out or obstructing the field.

Dismissals

  • Bowled, caught, lbw, stumped and hit wicket credit the bowler and need the striker as the batter out.
  • Run out and obstructing the field are not credited to the bowler; either batter can be out.
  • On a wide: only run out, stumped, hit wicket, obstructing. On a no-ball, bye or leg-bye: only run out or obstructing.
  • Retired hurt (can return, not a wicket), retired out, and timed out (new batter not ready in time).
  • A caught batter is replaced at the striker's end (batters do not cross).

Players and extra players

  • Up to 13 players a side. An innings ends when all but one of them are out (10 wickets for 11 a side, 12 for 13).
  • Up to 2 extra players per side. They only field: they can take catches and run people out, but cannot bat or bowl.

Bowlers

  • No bowler may bowl two consecutive overs.
  • Limited overs: a bowler may bowl at most one-fifth of the innings (T20: 4, ODI: 10, rounded up for custom lengths). Super Over: 1 over. Tests: no limit.

Powerplay (fielding restrictions)

  • T20: overs 1-6 maximum 2 fielders outside the 30-yard circle, then 5.
  • ODI: overs 1-10 (2 outside), 11-40 (4 outside), 41-50 (5 outside). Other lengths are scaled proportionally.

Test matches

  • Up to 5 days, minimum 90 overs per day, no overs limit per innings, declarations allowed, new ball available after 80 overs.
  • Follow-on offered when the first-innings lead is 200 (5+ days), 150 (3-4 days), 100 (2 days) or 75 (1 day).
  • Results: win by runs, wickets, or an innings and runs; tie; draw (close of play or days used up).

Results

  • Won by runs or wickets. Scores level in limited overs goes to a Super Over (1 over each, 2 wickets); repeated until there is a winner.
  • Rain or other interruptions: enter the revised overs and DLS target from your official DLS calculator under "More actions". The scorer does not calculate DLS itself.
